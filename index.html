Description

Sherry FitzGerald Crowley are delighted to bring to the market this impressive five bedroom detached property, set on an elevated south facing site offering panoramic views across Clew Bay and some of its islands to Croagh Patrick.

The spacious and well proportioned house was built circa 2000. Some of its quality finishes include timber floors and a beech kitchen. The floor area extends to approximately 225.2 sq m (2,424 sq ft). It would make a superb family residence or an exceptional home away from home holiday retreat.

On the ground floor, there is a large entrance hall, a light filled living room featuring east, south and west facing windows to maximise the sea and island views and catch day long sunlight. The dining room also benefits from dual aspect sea views and opens into the well appointed kitchen with an adjoining utility room. The main bedroom has south facing patio doors overlooking the sea, a roomy walk in wardrobe and an en-suite shower room.

On the first floor there are four double bedrooms (three with en-suite shower rooms) and a bathroom. The bedrooms all have wonderful sea or mountain views.

Externally, the gardens measure approximately 0.69 of an acre fronted by stone walls and wrought iron gates. There are extensive lawns to the front and side, a steeltech shed and a tarmac driveway with ample parking to the rear of the house.

Sandhill is a peaceful countryside area, located 8.5 km from Mulranny, an attractive coastal village on the Wild Atlantic Way, whose many amenities include primary and pre schools, a Blue Flag Beach, hotel, restaurants/ bars, shops, playground and nine hole golf course. It is a 10 km drive to Newport, a small town with a good range of facilities including primary school, shops, restaurants/bars and hotel. The award winning Nevins Bar & Restaurant is situated 2 km from the house and one of Mayo's finest leisure facilities, The Great Western Greenway cycle/walking trail running from Westport to Achill Island, is just 1 km away.
